NEWTOWN SQUARE, PA, October 25, 2026 (GLOBE
NEWSWIRE) -- ArriVent BioPharma, Inc. (Company or ArriVent) (Nasdaq: AVBP), a clinical-stage company dedicated to accelerating the
global development of innovative biopharmaceutical therapeutics, today announced initial encouraging Phase 1 results for ARR-217, (MRG007),
a cadherin-17 (CDH17)-targeting antibody-drug conjugate (ADC) developed in partnership with Lepu Biopharma Co., Ltd., in patients
with gastrointestinal malignancies. The findings were presented as part of an oral presentation at the 2026 European Society for Medical
Oncology (ESMO) Congress 2026.

"These initial results reinforce our confidence
in ARR-217 and in CDH17 as a potential therapeutic target for patients with gastrointestinal cancers who face limited treatment options
following prior lines of therapy," said Bing Yao, Ph.D., Chief Executive Officer of ArriVent. "We believe the responses observed
in this heavily pretreated patient population, together with a manageable safety profile, provided a strong foundation for advancing ARR-217
into a Phase 1b dose optimization study. As the first ADC from our pipeline to enter clinical development, ARR-217 represents an important
milestone for our ADC pipeline, and we look forward to dose optimization data expected in 2027."

The ongoing multicenter, open-label Phase 1 study
(NCT07066657) conducted in the US and China is evaluating the safety, tolerability, efficacy, and pharmacokinetics of ARR-217 in patients
with unresectable, locally advanced, or metastatic solid tumors, with an initial clinical development focus in colorectal, pancreatic,
and other gastrointestinal (GI) cancers. As of the August 5, 2026 data cutoff, 123 patients with heavily pretreated GI malignancies
were treated across 6 dose levels ranging from 2 mg/kg to 8 mg/kg, including 113 patients with Colorectal Cancer (CRC).

Key Highlights for ARR-217 Phase 1 Study Include:

·

Strong Anti-tumor Activity with ARR-217 at
Multiple Dose Levels

o

At 6 mg/kg Q3W dose, 44.8% (13/29) objective response rate (ORR) observed in pre-treated CRC patients,
including 7 confirmed partial responses, 4 partial responses pending confirmation, and 2 unconfirmed partial responses

o

At 6 mg/kg Q3W dose, 93.1% (27/29) disease control rate (DCR) observed

o

At doses 4 mg/kg Q3W and higher, 34.2% (25/73) ORR observed in CRC patients, including 15 confirmed partial
responses, 5 partial responses pending confirmation and 5 unconfirmed partial responses

o

At doses 4 mg/kg Q3W and higher, 91.8% DCR observed

o

Majority of the responding patients remained on study treatment as of the August 5, 2026 data cutoff

·

Generally, Well Tolerated Safety Profile

o

The most common treatment-related adverse events were Grade 1/2

o

Across all doses, 30.1% (37/123) patients experienced Grade 3 or higher treatment-related adverse events,
including neutropenia (12.2%) and diarrhea (8.9%)

o

At 6.0 mg/kg Q3W dose, Grade 3 or higher TRAE was 20.4% (10/49) including 2.0% diarrhea (1/49)

o

No grade 5 TRAE or ILD observed

Dose Optimization and Next Steps

ARR-217 has advanced into Phase 1b dose optimization
at doses of 5 and 6 mg/kg Q3W with further dose optimization data expected in 2027.

About ARR-217

ARR-217 (also known as MRG007) is a potentially
best-in-class cadherin-17 (CDH17)-targeted antibody-drug conjugate (ADC), utilizing a glycan-linked, exatecan-based payload. CDH17 is
a membranous cell adhesion molecule frequently overexpressed in colorectal cancer and several other gastrointestinal cancers, with limited
expression in normal intestinal tissue and pancreatic duct, making it an attractive target for ADC therapy. ArriVent obtained exclusive
rights to develop and commercialize ARR-217 worldwide outside of Greater China under a collaboration agreement with Lepu Biopharma Co., Ltd.
ARR-217 is the lead clinical candidate from ArriVent's growing ADC portfolio.

About ArriVent

ArriVent is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical
company dedicated to the identification, development, and commercialization of differentiated medicines to address the unmet medical needs
of patients with cancers. ArriVent seeks to utilize its team’s deep drug development experience to maximize the potential of its
lead development candidate, firmonertinib, and advance a pipeline of novel therapeutics, such as next-generation antibody drug conjugates,
through approval and commercialization.
