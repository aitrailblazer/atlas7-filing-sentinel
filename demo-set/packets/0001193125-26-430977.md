Item 8.01

Other Events. 

On October 26, 2026, Phathom Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (“Phathom” or the “Company”) announced the U.S. Food and Drug Administration’s (“FDA’s”) establishment of a public docket (Docket No. FDA-2026-N-13038) to solicit comment on legal and regulatory questions regarding certain issues raised by a filer of an abbreviated new drug application (“ANDA”) referencing VOQUEZNA (vonoprazan) tablets, BDR Pharmaceuticals, Intl. Pvt. Ltd. (“BDR”), in litigation brought against the FDA following FDA’s rejection of the ANDA. The litigation was brought to challenge FDA’s June 6, 2025 decision granting Phathom’s Citizen Petition, which agreed with Phathom that VOQUEZNA benefits from 10 years of New Chemical Entity (“NCE”) exclusivity through May 3, 2032. The deadline for comments submitted to the public docket is November 23, 2026. 

Phathom believes the FDA carefully reviewed the NCE exclusivity period applicable to VOQUEZNA through a full public process and reached the right decision, which exclusivity remains in effect. Phathom will make that case clearly in its comments to the FDA and in any appropriate court proceeding. 

A second purported ANDA filer, Saba Ilac Sanayii Ve Ticaret AS, has filed a notice in the BDR litigation claiming to have a pending related case, and that it intends to move to consolidate its case with BDR’s.
