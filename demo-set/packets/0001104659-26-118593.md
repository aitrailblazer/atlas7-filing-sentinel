Item
7.01. Regulation FD Disclosure.

On October 26, 2026, Braemar
Hotels & Resorts Inc. (the “Company”) announced that it will nominate the following eight individuals for election as
directors of the Company at the Company's 2026 annual meeting of stockholders (the “Annual Meeting”).

Each of the following individuals
will be nominated for election as a director of the Company at the Annual Meeting, to be identified as the Company's nominees in the definitive
proxy statement to be filed by the Company in connection with the Annual Meeting (the “Proxy Statement”), and to serve until
the Company's next annual meeting of stockholders and until his or her successor is duly elected and qualifies: Craig A. Barbarosh, Kevin
Covey, Stephen Haggerty, Daniel J. Hirsch, Kevin M. Luebbers, Gail P. Steinel, Richard J. Stockton, and Stephen W. Theriot.

A brief biography of each nominee
is set forth below.

Craig A. Barbarosh

Mr. Barbarosh brings more than
30 years of experience advising boards and serving as a director of public companies, including real estate investment trusts. From October
2023 to September 2026, Mr. Barbarosh served as Senior Managing Director of CommonWealth Partners LLC, a vertically integrated real estate
investment, development and management firm with approximately $8 billion of assets under management, where he co-led one of the firm’s
investment strategies. From 2012 to 2023, Mr. Barbarosh was a partner at Katten Muchin Rosenman LLP, where he served as a member of the
firm’s Board of Directors and Executive and Operating Committee. From 1992 to 2012, Mr. Barbarosh was an associate and subsequently
a partner at Pillsbury Winthrop Shaw Pittman LLP, where he served as a member of the firm’s Board of Directors, chair of its Board
Strategy Committee and Managing Partner of its Orange County office. Throughout his legal career, Mr. Barbarosh has advised companies
and their boards on complex corporate transactions.

Mr. Barbarosh has extensive
public company board experience. He has served as a member of the Board of Directors of Sabra Health Care REIT, Inc. (NASDAQ: SBRA) since
2010, where he currently serves as chair of the Audit Committee and as a member of the Compensation Committee, and as a member of the
Board of Directors of Evolent Health, Inc. (NYSE: EVH) since 2020, where he currently serves as chair of the Strategy Committee and as
a member of the Compensation Committee. Mr. Barbarosh previously served as Vice Chairman of NextGen Healthcare, Inc. (NASDAQ: NXGN) from
2009 to 2023 and as Chairman of the Board of Lifecore Biomedical, Inc. (NASDAQ: LFCR) from 2019 to 2023.

Mr. Barbarosh received a Juris
Doctor, with honors, from the University of the Pacific, McGeorge School of Law and a Bachelor of Arts degree in Business Economics from
the University of California, Santa Barbara. He has also completed executive education programs in corporate valuation at The Wharton
School of the University of Pennsylvania, cybersecurity oversight at Carnegie Mellon University and corporate boards, strategic business
valuation and private equity and venture capital at Harvard Business School.

Kevin Covey

Mr. Covey is the founder and
Managing Partner of GrayStreet Partners, a real estate and private credit investment firm he established in 2009 under its predecessor,
Caliburn Capital. For more than 15 years, he has led the firm’s investment activities across commercial real estate, hospitality
and private credit, with responsibility for sourcing, underwriting, capital allocation, transaction structuring and portfolio oversight.

Real estate investment and ownership
are central to Mr. Covey’s career, including acquisitions, redevelopment, adaptive reuse and the repositioning of hospitality and
other commercial properties. He is an active principal in Ithaca Investments, Ltd., which owns more than 1.2 million square feet of retail
shopping center space in Texas.

Mr. Covey also brings deep credit
and capital markets experience. GrayStreet invests in high-yield debt instruments, and has structured financings, recapitalizations and
complex transactions across multiple asset classes, including situations requiring creative capital solutions.

Mr. Covey received Bachelor
of Arts degrees in Economics and Mathematics from the University of Chicago.

Stephen Haggerty

Mr. Haggerty brings more than
30 years of global hotel real estate, development and capital markets experience. He is the Principal and Founder of Bare Hill Advisory,
a real estate investment and advisory platform focused on hospitality and wellness-related assets, which he founded in 2021. From May
2018 to October 2021, Mr. Haggerty served as Managing Partner of Trinity Investments, a private investment platform focused on hospitality
real estate and related assets.

Mr. Haggerty spent approximately
11 years at Hyatt Hotels Corporation in several senior leadership positions. From 2014 to 2018, he served as Global Head of Franchising,
Select Service and Capital Strategy; from 2012 to 2014, he served as Global Head of Real Estate and Capital Strategy; and from 2007 to
2012, he served as Global Head of Real Estate and Development. Prior to joining Hyatt, Mr. Haggerty spent approximately 13 years at Marriott
International in a number of increasingly senior positions, including as Senior Vice President, Global Asset Management from 2003 to 2007
(based in London from 2005), Vice President, Development for the Asia-Pacific region from 1997 to 2003 and Vice President, Feasibility
from 1994 to 1997. Earlier in his career, Mr. Haggerty held positions with HVS International.

Mr. Haggerty served as a member
of the Board of Directors of Playa Hotels & Resorts (NASDAQ: PLYA) from 2013 to 2018 and of Broadmark Realty Capital Inc. (NYSE: BRMK)
from November 2019 to July 2023, where he served as chair of the Compensation Committee. He also served as a member of the Board of Directors
of B. Riley Securities from April 2025 to May 2026. Mr. Haggerty currently serves as a board member of Simply Resorts & Retreats,
a founding member of the advisory board of Parafin and a Senior Advisor and Managing Director of Devotio Capital Management. Mr. Haggerty
received a Bachelor of Science degree from Cornell University’s School of Hotel Administration.

Daniel J. Hirsch

Mr. Hirsch brings more than
two decades of real estate investment and public company board experience. From 2003 to 2016, Mr. Hirsch held several increasingly senior
positions at Farallon Capital Management, L.L.C., including Managing Member, Managing Director and Legal Counsel. From 2013 to 2016, he
co-led Farallon’s Real Estate Group, where he helped manage the firm’s real estate team and served as a member of its investment
committee, with responsibility for evaluating acquisition opportunities, negotiating transactions and joint ventures, asset and portfolio
management, fundraising and structuring investment vehicles. From 2009 to 2012, he helped manage a $3.5 billion multi-sector real estate
equity portfolio. Earlier in his tenure at Farallon, he worked on the negotiation, structuring and execution of private and public market
debt and equity transactions in the United States and internationally. Prior to joining Farallon, Mr. Hirsch was an associate at Covington
& Burling from 2001 to 2003.

Since August 2022, Mr. Hirsch
has served as an Executive-in-Residence at Anzu Partners, LLC, a venture capital firm focused on industrial and life science technology
companies. He served as Chief Financial Officer and a director of Anzu Special Acquisition Corp I from August 2022 to September 2023 and
as Chief Operating Officer and Chief Financial Officer of Cascade Acquisition Corp. from August 2020 to May 2022. From 2018 to 2025, Mr.
Hirsch was a principal of KRDH Holdings, LLC, a venture focused on the acquisition, entitlement and development of multifamily real estate
projects, and from 2017 to 2020 he advised Farallon Capital Management with respect to its investment in Playa Hotels & Resorts.

Mr. Hirsch has served as a member
of the Board of Directors of Macerich (NYSE: MAC) since June 2018, where he currently serves as a member of its Compensation Committee
and Capital Allocation Committee and previously served as chair of its Nominating and Corporate Governance Committee. He previously served
as a director of Playa Hotels & Resorts (NASDAQ: PLYA) from 2010 to 2020, where he served as chair of the Compensation Committee and
as a member of the Nominating and Corporate Governance and Capital Allocation Committees; Broadmark Realty Capital (NYSE: BRMK) from 2019
to 2023, where he served as chair of the Nominating and Corporate Governance Committee and as a member of the Compensation and Finance
Committees; Ready Capital Corporation (NYSE: RC) from 2023 to 2024; and Nuburu Inc. (NYSE American: BURU) from 2023 to 2025. He currently
serves as a member of the advisory board of Shore Capital Real Estate Partners.

Mr. Hirsch received a Juris
Doctor from Yale Law School and a Bachelor of Arts degree, summa cum laude, from Amherst College.

Kevin M. Luebbers

Mr. Luebbers brings approximately
30 years of hotel investment, public REIT finance and audit committee experience. He co-founded and has served since 2004 as a partner
of VIC Partners, LLC, an investment partnership focused on acquiring and repositioning hotel properties. From 2000 to 2003, Mr. Luebbers
served as Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer of RFS Hotel Investors, Inc., a publicly traded hotel real estate investment
trust, where he was responsible for the company’s capital markets and treasury functions. From 1996 to 2000, he served as Senior
Vice President of Planning and Investment Analysis at Hilton Hotels Corporation.

Mr. Luebbers served as a member
of the Board of Directors of Broadmark Realty Capital (NYSE: BRMK) from 2019 to 2023, where he served as chair of the Audit Committee,
and of Ready Capital Corporation (NYSE: RC) from 2023 to 2024. From 2005 to 2008, Mr. Luebbers served as a member of the Board of Directors
and chair of the Audit Committee of Ambassadors International, Inc. (NASDAQ: AMIE), a publicly traded cruise, marina, travel and events
company.

Mr. Luebbers received a Bachelor
of Science degree from Cornell University School of Hotel Administration and a Master of Business Administration from the University of
California, Berkeley.

Gail P. Steinel

Ms. Steinel brings approximately
20 years of public company board leadership, including extensive service as an audit committee chair. She currently serves as a Senior
Advisor to Alvarez & Marsal’s Global Transaction Advisory Group, which provides financial and operational due diligence services
to private equity firms and corporations, and as a Senior Advisor to Nichols Law, a government contracts law firm. Ms. Steinel began her
career as an auditor with Arthur Andersen and subsequently held a number of leadership positions with the firm, ultimately serving as
Global Managing Partner of its Business Consulting Group, where she led approximately 10,000 professionals across 85 countries. She later
led the transition of approximately 8,000 professionals to KPMG Consulting, which was subsequently renamed BearingPoint.

Ms. Steinel has served as a
member of the Board of Trustees of Federal Realty Investment Trust (NYSE: FRT) since 2006, where she currently serves as chair of the
Audit Committee and as a member of the Compensation Committee. She has also served as a member of the Board of Directors of Invesque Corporation
(TSX: IVQ) since 2021, where she serves as chair of the Audit Committee and as a member of the Compensation and Nominating Committees,
and as a director of DAI Corporation since 2006, where she has served as chair of its Audit Committee. From 2009 to 2020, she served as
a director of MTS Systems Corporation (NASDAQ: MTSC), where she served as chair of the Audit Committee, until the company’s acquisition
by Amphenol Corporation. In 2025, Ms. Steinel was named by The Wall Street Journal as one of its Top 250 Board Members.

Ms. Steinel received a Bachelor
of Science degree in Accounting from Rutgers University and has served as a guest lecturer on leadership at Yale University, Harvard University,
the United States Military Academy at West Point and Villanova University.

Richard J. Stockton

Mr. Stockton has served as the
Company’s Chief Executive Officer since November 2016, as President since April 2017 and as a member of the Board of Directors since
July 2020, and will continue to serve as a director if re-elected at the Annual Meeting. Upon completion of the Company’s separation
from Ashford Inc., Mr. Stockton will become employed directly by the Company; he was previously employed by Ashford Inc. from 2016.

Prior to joining the Company,
Mr. Stockton served as Global Co-Head and Global Chief Operating Officer for Real Estate at CarVal Investors, a subsidiary of Cargill
Inc., with real estate investments in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and France. He also previously served as President
& CEO-Americas for OUE Limited, a publicly listed Singaporean property company, where he established the business that acquired and
refurbished the US Bank Tower in Los Angeles in 2013. The majority of his career, more than 15 years, was spent at Morgan Stanley in real
estate investment banking in various roles, including Head of EMEA Real Estate Banking in London and Co-Head of Asia Pacific Real Estate
Banking.

He served as the Lead Independent
Director of Spirit MTA REIT (NYSE: SMTA) and Trustee of its successor entity, SMTA Liquidating Trust, from 2018 to 2025.

He is a member of the Board
of the American Hotel & Lodging Association and is a frequent speaker at industry conferences, including NAREIT, the NYU International
Hospitality Industry Investment Conference and the Americas Lodging Investment Summit.

Mr. Stockton received a Master
of Business Administration degree in Finance and Real Estate from The Wharton School, University of Pennsylvania, and a Bachelor of Science
degree from Cornell University’s School of Hotel Administration.

Stephen W. Theriot

Mr. Theriot brings more than
40 years of finance, accounting, capital allocation and corporate governance experience with publicly traded real estate companies. From
2017 to 2021, Mr. Theriot served as the Chief Financial Officer of JBG SMITH Properties, a NYSE-listed REIT and an S&P 400 company
that owns, operates, invests in and develops a portfolio of mixed-use properties in and around Washington, D.C., where he was responsible
for the accounting, tax, investor relations and information technology functions and, in conjunction with JBG SMITH’s spin-off from
Vornado Realty Trust, directed the development of its stand-alone teams and processes. From 2021 to 2022, he served as a Senior Advisor
to JBG SMITH’s leadership team.

From 2013 to 2017, Mr. Theriot
served as Chief Financial Officer of Vornado Realty Trust, an S&P 500 REIT, where he directed the accounting, tax, investor relations
and information technology functions, served as deal captain for the spin-off of Vornado’s Washington, D.C. segment and the formation
of JBG SMITH, and helped direct the spin-off of Urban Edge Properties. Prior to joining Vornado, he spent approximately 30 years in Deloitte’s
Audit & Assurance group, culminating in his appointment as Partner in charge of Deloitte’s Northeast Region Real Estate Practice,
serving REITs, home builders and hospitality and gaming enterprises. He also served as Deputy Professional Practice Director, advising
clients on the application of GAAP and SEC regulations to complex merger, acquisition and capital markets transactions, and as Partner
in charge of Deloitte’s Atlanta/Birmingham Audit & Assurance practices.

Mr. Theriot has served as a
Senior Advisor to Plumbrook Capital Partners, a real estate investment firm focused on middle-market multifamily real estate investments,
since 2024 and as a member of the Board of Directors of the Treasury Department Federal Credit Union in Washington, D.C. since 2023, including
as Vice Chairman from 2024 to 2026. Mr. Theriot received a Bachelor of Science degree from the University of North Carolina at Chapel
Hill.

Inc. (NYSE: BHR) (“Braemar” or the “Company”) today announced that its Board of Directors (the “Board”)
intends to nominate eight highly qualified, seasoned leaders for election at the Company’s 2026 Annual Meeting of Stockholders (the
“Annual Meeting”). The slate of eight director candidates includes seven new, highly qualified and independent director
nominees:

·

Craig A. Barbarosh was Senior Managing
Director of CommonWealth Partners, an $8 billion real estate investment firm, and has served on the board of Sabra Health Care REIT since
2010, where he chairs the Audit Committee. He also chairs the Strategy Committee at Evolent Health and previously served as Vice Chairman
of NextGen Healthcare and Chairman of Lifecore Biomedical. He holds a BA from the University of California, Santa Barbara and a JD from
the University of the Pacific McGeorge School of Law.

·

Kevin Covey is the founder and Managing
Partner of GrayStreet Partners, a real estate and private credit investment firm, where for more than 15 years he has led investments
spanning commercial real estate, hospitality and credit. He is also a principal in Ithaca Investments, which owns more than 1.2 million
square feet of Texas retail property. He holds BA degrees in Economics and Mathematics from the University of Chicago.

·

Stephen Haggerty spent more than two decades
in senior real estate, development and capital strategy roles at Marriott and Hyatt, where he served as Global Head of Real Estate and
Development, and later served as Managing Partner of hotel investment platform Trinity Investments. He previously served on the boards
of Playa Hotels & Resorts and Broadmark Realty Capital and holds a BS from Cornell University’s School of Hotel Administration.

·

Daniel J. Hirsch co-led the real estate
group at Farallon Capital and has served on the board of directors of Macerich since 2018. He previously served on the boards of Playa
Hotels & Resorts, where he chaired the Compensation Committee, and Broadmark Realty Capital. He holds a BA from Amherst College and
a JD from Yale Law School.

·

Kevin M. Luebbers served as CFO of RFS
Hotel Investors, a public hotel REIT, and as a senior vice president at Hilton Hotels before co-founding hotel investment firm VIC Partners.
He previously chaired the Audit Committee of Broadmark Realty Capital and holds a BS from Cornell University’s School of Hotel Administration
and an MBA from the University of California, Berkeley.

·

Gail P. Steinel has
been a trustee of Federal Realty Investment Trust since 2006, where she chairs the Audit Committee, and in 2025 was named one of The
Wall Street Journal’s Top 250 Board Members. She previously served as Global Managing Partner of Arthur Andersen’s Business
Consulting Group and is a Senior Advisor to Alvarez & Marsal’s Global Transaction Advisory Group. She holds a BS in Accounting
from Rutgers University.

·

Richard J. Stockton has been CEO of Braemar
since 2016 and previously spent more than 15 years at Morgan Stanley, where he ran real estate investment banking for Europe, the Middle
East and Africa. He also served as Lead Independent Director of Spirit MTA REIT and holds a BS from Cornell University’s School
of Hotel Administration and an MBA from the Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania.

A REFRESHED, INDEPENDENT BOARD

This significant refreshment reflects the Board’s
commitment to independent, stockholder-focused governance following the termination of the Company’s external advisory relationship
with Ashford Inc. and its affiliates (together, “Ashford”).

With the sole exception of Richard J. Stockton,
the Company’s President and Chief Executive Officer who provides essential institutional knowledge, every incumbent director tied
to the legacy Ashford relationship is stepping aside, clearing the way for a Board composed overwhelmingly of new, independent directors.
In addition, once seated, the new Board of Directors will select an independent Chairman. Ferguson Partners conducted the executive search
process and recommended the new independent directors for nomination at the Company’s next annual meeting.

“Braemar is entering a new chapter, and
our board slate reflects that reality,” said Mr. Stockton. “These nominees are accomplished leaders with deep experience in
REIT oversight, hotel investment, capital markets, finance and strategic transactions. Following the termination of the Ashford advisory
agreement, Braemar will have a refreshed board that is independent, highly qualified and focused on maximizing value for all stockholders.”
